# 雷峰夕照 (Leifeng Pagoda at Sunset): the lady in white, 法海, and the crab

The real place:
- Leifeng Pagoda stands on 夕照山 on the south shore. Qian Chu, King of
  Wuyue, finished it around 977.
- In the Ming (嘉靖) its wooden eaves burned, leaving only the brick core:
  「雷峰如老衲，保俶如美人」.
- People believed its bricks warded off harm and dug them out one by one.
  The old pagoda fell on 25 September 1924, and Lu Xun wrote
  《论雷峰塔的倒掉》.
- Its bricks held hidden sutra scrolls.
- In 2001 the underground palace (地宫) was opened, and a solid silver Ashoka
  stupa holding a relic of the Buddha's hair was found.
- The new pagoda of 2002 stands on the same spot, with the ruins kept under
  glass in its base, and escalators and a lift to the top.
- Jingci Temple (南屏晚钟) is just across the road.

In the legend of the White Snake, 法海 imprisons 白娘子 under the pagoda:
「雷峰塔倒，白蛇出世」. A folk tale says he finally hid inside a crab shell,
which is why every crab has a cross-legged "little monk" inside (蟹和尚, the
stomach).

1. **Dusk at the foot of the hill.** The pagoda is golden in the setting sun.
   - The lady in white from Dream 5 returns and reveals she is Bai Suzhen.
   - She has never watched the sunset from the top.
   - 法海 jumps out from behind a willow, then runs up the hill.
2. **The steps.** A side-scrolling race up 110 steps.
   - Alternate ←/→ (left foot, right foot) to climb; the same foot twice
     trips you.
   - Jump over crabs and wooden fish (木鱼) that 法海 sends rolling down.
   - GOGO and the lady glide up the escalator alongside, and you race them.
3. **The ruins in the base.** Under glass, 法海's crab minions pry bricks out
   of the old pagoda's wall and carry them off.
   - Tap a crab to make it drop its brick, then tap the brick to put it back.
     Some bricks hold sutra scrolls.
   - Afterwards the underground palace glows and the silver stupa rises.
   - The lift works again.
4. **The top at sunset.** A golden panorama: 苏堤, 三潭印月, 保俶塔 ("the
   beauty") and egrets flying home.
   - Take the photo when the sun just touches the western hills. You get
     three shots.
   - 法海 keeps photobombing: tap him away.
   - Then he is cornered on the railing and, poof, hides in a crab shell.
   - The 净慈寺 bell rings, and the lady floats off towards the Broken Bridge.
5. **Waking up.** Dinner is a plate of hairy crabs. GOGO opens one and finds
   the little monk: "法海! So this is where you've been hiding!" Mum: it's
   the crab's stomach, don't eat it. The 夕照 scroll and the invitation
   follow.

**Art:** The new pagoda, sky, sun, lady, 法海, crabs, bricks and silver stupa
are painted on canvases with gradients. The pagoda has three-faced
octagonal storeys, bronze tiled eaves, lit doorways and a sunset wash. Tree
crowns have warm rims on the sun side.

**Music:**

- `lfRuins`: low guzheng.
- `lfSunset`: broad flute.
- `lfHome`: bells.
- New sound effects: wooden-fish tock, poof, crab scuttle, boing and the
