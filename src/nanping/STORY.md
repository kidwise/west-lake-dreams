# 南屏晚钟 (Evening Bell at Nanping Hill): the bell that lost its voice

The real place:
- 净慈寺 stands below 慧日峰 of 南屏山, across the road from Leifeng Pagoda.
  Qian Chu of Wuyue built it in 954 for the Chan master 永明延寿.
- The old bell is long gone, and the "evening bell" was silent for almost a
  century.
- In 1986 a new bronze bell was cast: about three metres tall and over ten
  tonnes, carrying more than 68,000 characters of the Lotus Sutra. That
  November it rang 108 times.
- Every New Year's Eve the temple rings the bell 108 times, and the last
  stroke brings in the New Year.
- Two traditions are used here, both presented as legend:
  - 济公 brought timber up from a well to rebuild the temple (the 运木古井
    is still there).
  - The hill's rocks are full of hollows, so the bell's sound echoes and
    carries far.

Ten Scenes is the one scene you *hear*, so the chapter is built around sound.

1. **The mute bell.** At dusk in 净慈寺, GOGO swings the old striker: the bell
   only goes "pff" in a puff of dust, and the rotten log snaps.
   - 济公 pops out from behind the bell, scruffy and cheerful: torn cap, holey
     fan, wine gourd. Half the bell's voice has run off into the caves of
     南屏山.
   - His aside about 法海 depends on whether you've played 雷峰夕照: "still
     hiding in that crab shell" or "a very serious monk across the road".
2. **The old log well.** Seen from above, things rise out of the dark water.
   - Grab them the moment they surface: too early and they slip, too late and
     they sink.
   - Logs only. 济公's shoe, his wine gourd and a crab come up too, with jokes.
   - Then pick the straight log for the striker over the bent and the cracked
     one.
3. **The echoes of 南屏山.** A listen-and-repeat memory game.
   - Five caves each sing a pentatonic note in its own colour.
   - 济公 taps his wooden fish, the caves sing a phrase of 3, 5, then 7 notes,
     and you sing it back (tap or press 1–5).
   - Wrong notes get a hiccup. Right ones send the lost "voices" flying home.
   - The background music stops so the notes can be heard.
4. **Ringing the bell.** Hold to pull the striker back and release to strike.
   - Strength must land in the gold zone, and the echo must fade below a mark
     before the next strike.
   - 108 strokes would take all night, so in the dream each good strike counts
     for twelve.
   - Each good strike sends a ring across the lake to another of the Ten
     Scenes. **The roll call depends on which chapters the player has
     already dreamt**, because chapters can be played in any order.
     - A visited scene: an old friend answers, such as 白娘子's umbrella, 牛奶奶's
       moo, leaping koi, the Jade Rabbit, or 法海's crab.
     - An unvisited scene: it glows with a "?" and the line "What story is
       waiting there?"
   - At 108 everyone answers at once, and that picture goes on the scroll.
5. **Waking up: New Year's Eve.** Firecrackers outside and the real bell of
   净慈寺 across the lake count 105, 106, 107, 108. "Happy New Year!" and
   fireworks follow.
   - Mum: "after New Year, it's spring".
   - A few willow buds outside the window: a seasonal close, not a pointer to
     a next chapter.

**Art:**
- 济公 is painted at twice the resolution in picture-book style, with ink
  outlines.
  - His robe is patched and stitched, with a torn hem and a rope sash.
  - His tilted cap has a red patch and a hole showing his bald head.
  - He waves a holey palm-leaf fan and carries a shiny wine gourd with a red
    cord.
  - He has a stubbly grin and a wine-red nose.
- The four backgrounds are painted once as large canvases.
  - **The temple at dusk:** layered forest with mist pooling below, two old
    camphor trees, and lantern light on the stones.
  - **The well from above:** a wooden bucket, a coil of rope, ferns and fallen
    leaves.
  - **The cliff:** built from weathered, mossy blocks with ragged caves.
  - **The bell tower:** wood grain, red lanterns, and evening light falling
    across the floor.
- The New Year's Eve room has curtains, narcissus, a red tablecloth and
  fireworks.

**Music:**
- `npDusk`: guzheng.
- `npWell`: pipa and woodblock.
- `npBell`: low 箫, with room for the bell.
- `npNewYear`: festive.
- New sound effects: a synthesized great temple bell (good, dull or harsh)
  and a hiccup.

Other chapters were also made order-independent:
- In 雷峰夕照, the lady in white says "we meet again" only if 断桥残雪 has been
  played.
- In 双峰插云, the closing line about 南屏山's bell no longer sounds like a
  "next chapter" teaser.
